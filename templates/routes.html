    How the estimate works

    Fuel cost = distance ÷ your mileage × fuel price. OSRM supplies candidate road paths, geometry and turn instructions. The rule-based route scorer combines your time/fuel priority, available traffic and (when selected) equal-weight proximity to each chosen service category. It compares only returned candidate paths; it does not guarantee a globally optimal route or a guaranteed stop at every service. Traffic sampling is sparse and the adjusted ETA is approximate. Service distance is a straight-line estimate to the route geometry, not a driving detour. Available places are the named records loaded in CITY, not a complete nationwide city catalogue.
